# Brief: customer case study for Harborlane (FICTIONAL vendor, customer and numbers)

Customer: Brightwell Foods, regional refrigerated-food distributor, ~900 employees, Midwest US.
Vendor: Harborlane (freight invoice audit software).
Challenge: 14,000 carrier invoices a month, audited by one analyst sampling ~5%; accessorial charges kept climbing;
overcharges discovered after payment, past the carriers' 180-day claims window.
Solution: connected TMS + carrier EDI in 9 days; re-rates every line against contracts pre-payment; auto-disputes.
Results: freight overbilling per year dropped from $1.9M to $1.1M... (use computed -42%), audit coverage 5% -> 100%
(20x), invoice approval time from 12 days to 3 days (-75%).
Quote: "We used to find mistakes after the money was gone. Now they are fixed before the invoice reaches my desk."
 - Marcus Lindqvist, Controller, Brightwell Foods
