# Brief: Harborlane seed deck (FICTIONAL company and numbers, for demonstration)

Company: Harborlane. Freight-invoice audit software for mid-market US shippers.
Tagline: "Stop paying freight bills you never agreed to."
Round: $4.0M seed. Audience: seed-stage VCs. Date: Nov 2026. Contact: founders@harborlane.example

Problem: Mid-market shippers get thousands of carrier invoices a month. Contracted rates, accessorials and
duplicate charges are checked by one analyst in spreadsheets, after the bill is already paid. Industry audits
find roughly 8% of invoices contain a billing error; claims windows (often 180 days) lapse.
Solution: Connects to the TMS/ERP and carrier EDI feeds, re-rates every invoice line against the signed
contract before payment, auto-files disputes, and shows savings in a CFO dashboard.
Why now: carriers moved to dynamic accessorial pricing (2023); EDI 210 and API invoice feeds are now standard;
rate volatility after 2024 put freight on CFO radar; LLM document parsing makes contract ingest cheap.
Market: 38,000 US shippers with $20M+ freight spend; 11,400 use parcel/LTL/truckload through a TMS we integrate
with; we expect 570 customers (5% of SAM) within five years. ACV $24,000.
Traction: MRR by month (USD K) Dec-25..Nov-26: 8, 11, 15, 19, 24, 30, 37, 45, 54, 64, 76, 90. 41 customers,
net revenue retention 128%, gross margin 78%, 3 logos above $60K ACV.
Business model: annual SaaS, tiers Starter $9K, Growth $24K (most popular), Enterprise from $60K.
Unit economics: CAC $14K, payback 7 months, gross margin 78%, NRR 128%.
Competition: legacy audit firms (contingency fee), TMS built-in audit modules, in-house spreadsheets, AP automation suites.
GTM: land via controller/logistics-manager outbound + 3PL partners; expand to more carriers/entities; scale with
TMS marketplace and channel.
Team: CEO Dana Whitlock (ex-VP finance ops, national retailer), CTO Ravi Menon (ex-staff eng, payments), COO Leila Haddad (ex-3PL operations), Head of Sales Tom Brandt (built mid-market sales at two logistics SaaS firms).
